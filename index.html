About System

An introduction to the PHOTO COFFEE project.

PHOTO COFFEE

PHOTO COFFEE is a coffee identification system concept developed using digital image processing, computer vision, and deep learning. The project focuses on visual identification of coffee bean varieties.

Research Objective

To explore the development of a computer vision-based system for identifying Arabika, Ekselsa, Liberika, and Robusta coffee beans through digital images.

Classification Categories

Arabika, Ekselsa, Liberika, and Robusta.

Technology

Python, YOLOv8 Segmentation, Digital Image Processing, Computer Vision, and Web Interface.

Research Development

This project is part of an undergraduate research development initiative in Food Science and Technology, integrating agricultural commodities with artificial intelligence and visual analysis.
