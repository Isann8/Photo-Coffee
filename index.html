About System

An introduction to the PHOTO COFFEE research project.

PHOTO COFFEE

PHOTO COFFEE is a coffee identification system developed using digital image processing, computer vision, and deep learning. The system focuses on identifying green coffee bean varieties through digital images.

Research Objective

To develop a computer vision-based system for detecting and classifying Arabika, Ekselsa, Liberika, and Robusta coffee beans using YOLOv8 Instance Segmentation.

Classification Categories

The system supports four coffee bean varieties: Arabika, Ekselsa, Liberika, and Robusta.

Model Architecture

YOLOv8 Instance Segmentation is used to detect individual coffee bean objects, generate segmentation masks, and classify the detected objects into their corresponding categories.

Technology

Python, Streamlit, Ultralytics YOLOv8, OpenCV, NumPy, Pandas, and Pillow.

Application Features

Image upload, camera capture, live webcam detection, segmentation visualization, detection analytics, and detection history.

Research Development

This project is part of an undergraduate research development initiative in Food Science and Technology, integrating agricultural commodities with artificial intelligence and computer vision.
